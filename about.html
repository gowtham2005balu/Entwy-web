About Entwy

Where design meets intelligence.

Entwy is a design-led product engineering company. We help startups and businesses design, build, and
ship digital products that look sharp, work flawlessly, and scale without breaking — combining UI/UX
design, full-stack product engineering, and AI integration under one team.


We are a subsidiary of Zuntra Digital, working with technology companies, startups, and enterprise brands
across multiple industries worldwide.

Our Approach

Design and engineering don't happen in separate lanes at Entwy. The same team that maps your user journey builds the interface, ships the code, and wires in the AI.

That means fewer handoffs, fewer translation errors between "what was designed" and "what got built," and a product that feels considered at every layer — not stitched together from a dozen vendors.
